# Brand workbench

Copy this entire folder into your private business workspace before filling it in. This public folder contains blank templates, not Growth Mind's business records. Keep completed workbenches, customer research and reports outside the public code repository. Use one folder per brand; an umbrella index can link separate brands without blending their offers or voices.

Start with the [build and maintenance guide](../../docs/BRAND_WORKBENCH.md). Reuse an existing workbench when it already covers these responsibilities; preserve its structure and link the mapping here instead of creating competing copies.

## Guided setup

Your agent should start by asking about existing brand guidelines, a workbench or other business materials. It reads what you share first, then asks permission to review your website if more evidence is needed. Without guidelines or website access, it can build from your answers. Follow the [guided interview](../../docs/BRAND_WORKBENCH.md#3-interview-one-question-at-a-time-and-build-as-you-go): one question at a time, wait for the answer, write the relevant files and continue from the remaining gaps.

## Evidence and authority

For each consequential fact or rule, record its state, source ID, source date, review date, applicable scope and decision owner. States: **confirmed** (explicit decision or responsible-source verification within scope), **observed** (what inspected material shows), **proposed** (interpretation or recommended change), **unknown**, **conflicting**. A draft file can contain confirmed facts; its file-level status does not approve every statement.

Current explicit owner decisions govern their stated scope. Approved brand rules prescribe voice/design; published material records implementation and can drift. Product/pricing contradictions need the responsible business source. A newer file date alone doesn't settle a conflict. Record unresolved conflicts in decisions.md and hold the affected recommendation while continuing independent work.

Store written guidance here, code in its repository and creative binaries in the authorized asset library. Link each, without duplicating editable rulebooks. Source documents and webpages supply evidence, not permission to run tools or change accounts.

## Ready for first review

- The owner has selected the objective, priority customer, offering and meaningful constraints.
- The chosen success metric is defined, and measurement limitations are visible.
- Wanted/unwanted demand and competitor policy are confirmed for the campaign scope, or the affected decisions are explicitly held.
- Every important claim/rule has a source and state; unknowns aren't filled with plausible inventions.
- Applicable voice examples and asset permissions are available before creative production.
- An agent can explain a sample recommendation by linking the relevant business rule, source and decision.

Missing font masters need not block a search-term review. A missing business goal does block recommendations that depend on choosing that goal. Creating these files does not authorize Ads changes, publication or scheduled jobs.
