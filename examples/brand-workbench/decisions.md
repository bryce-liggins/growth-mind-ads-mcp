## Conflict handling
Show both sources and their scope. A current explicit decision can settle its stated scope; publication time alone cannot. Ask one focused question when authority or intent is unresolved, then update all affected current files. Preserve the previous rule as dated history. An agent recommendation or repeated use never becomes an owner decision automatically.

## Account-change boundary
Strategic decisions guide proposals. Approval of an exact Ads mutation batch and its execution receipt belong in the private change review. This file does not replace that approval protocol.
