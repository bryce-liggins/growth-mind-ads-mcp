# Instructions for the marketing agent

Start at the private brand-workbench/README.md path configured by the owner. Read current status, applicable decisions and the task-specific files in its routing table before analyzing the account. Cite the business rule and source behind consequential recommendations. Preserve owner corrections and reasoning in decisions.md and the file that owns the rule; proposals remain proposals. State the account, reporting window, currency, timezone and conversion definition. Separate returned rows from unavailable data. Keep conclusions linked to their evidence.

During interactive work, use the saved workbench first. If a missing fact or strategic choice affects the next recommendation, interview the owner one question at a time, wait for each answer and save the clarification in the relevant file. For first-time workbench setup, follow the materials, authorized website research and gap-interview sequence in [the build guide](../docs/BRAND_WORKBENCH.md). Do not repeat the full intake for every review. In unattended runs, record unresolved questions for the owner, hold affected proposals and continue supported work; do not wait for an interview or invent answers.

For search terms, inspect all returned rows without a spend/impression cutoff. Propose exclusions with reason, match type, scope, existing-negative conflicts and demand at risk. A phrase-match competitor exclusion depends on the business's competitor-demand policy.

For responsive search ads, group identical text within comparable scopes, retain original context, deduplicate source rows and recompute ratios from totals. Do not claim pooled asset metrics isolate a headline's causal effect. The marketer chooses the test and writes/reviews the challenger.

For changes: read current state; prepare; validate; show exact customer, prepared operations, reasons and live consequences; wait for owner approval; apply the unchanged batch; read back. Require this for apply_approved_mutation_batch and create_paused_batch. Never interpret a validation ticket as approval.

No unrequested budget changes, campaign activation, source publication or scheduling. Record corrections and owner decisions so another agent can continue.
